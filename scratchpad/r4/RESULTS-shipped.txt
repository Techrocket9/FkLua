THE SHIPPED fk.bulk_get, measured after it landed.
=================================================

Everything in RESULTS.txt is a PROTOTYPE measured beside the real dispatcher,
which is what a design round produces. These are the legs that call
H.bulk_get -- the function runtime/lua/fk_abi.lua ships and a packaged mod
runs -- plus the end-to-end figures through a real compiled guest.

  python3 scratchpad/r4/bench.py            (host side, bin/lua52f)
  go test ./internal/guest/ -run TestWhatAHostCallCostsThroughARealGuest -count=1

=== WHY THE SHIPPED FAST ARM IS 0.22x AND THE PROTOTYPE REACHED 0.044x ===
  The prototype dropped two things the shipped one keeps, and both are
  contracts rather than overhead.

    the per-element pcall     ~87 ns/element (RESULTS.txt, bulk_batchpcall
                              against bulk_full). It is what makes "an element
                              that raises costs its own element and the walk
                              continues" true; one pcall for the batch abandons
                              elements i+1..n.
    the store funnel          ~64 ns/element (bulk_store_u32 at 73.6 against a
                              hoisted array write). st32 marks the dirty page,
                              which is --persist=packed's save set AND
                              --gc=collected's write barrier. MEMPACK.mark is
                              inside the generated chunk and fk_abi.lua cannot
                              reach it, so a hoisted store would be the defect
                              class CLAUDE.md gates the inlined 8-byte store on.

  216.8 - 87 - 64 = 66 ns, against the prototype's 41-45 ns hoisted arm plus
  the valid check. The staircase closes.
